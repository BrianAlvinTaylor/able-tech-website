Crazy Train Casino Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Crazy Train Casino iPhone app from ABLE Tech LLC.

Game data on your iPhone

The app stores game progress, preferences, your virtual wallet, tickets, and Players Club activity on your iPhone. Optional Player Card information, such as your name and city, stays with your game data. The app has no ABLE Tech account, cloud sync, analytics service, or advertising trackers.

Apple purchases and links

Apple processes optional purchases through the App Store. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit service to verify and restore purchase access. ABLE Tech does not receive your card details. App Store links open Apple services, which are covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local game data stays in the app until you remove it by deleting the app from your iPhone. Deleting the app does not erase purchases from your Apple Account. Apple manages device backups and purchase history according to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies.
