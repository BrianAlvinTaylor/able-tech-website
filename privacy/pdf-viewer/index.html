ABLE Tech PDF Viewer Privacy Policy

How ABLE Tech PDF Viewer handles PDFs, markup, saved copies and support correspondence.

Effective date: October 29, 2026

ABLE Tech PDF Viewer is a macOS PDF viewing and light-markup app provided by ABLE Tech LLC. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Your PDFs and markup

The app accesses PDF files you choose through macOS and processes their contents, filenames, page order and annotations on your Mac to provide viewing, markup, saving copies and printing. These features do not upload your documents to ABLE Tech, and ABLE Tech does not receive your PDF contents or markup through the app.

Markup on an original page is placed on an editable page copy. Save Copy creates a separate PDF rather than overwriting the source file. Saved copies remain in the location you choose and include the document's current page order and annotations.

Accounts, advertising and tracking

The app does not require an account, display advertising, or include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not collect personal information, document contents or usage analytics for ABLE Tech through its PDF viewing and markup features.

Storage and services you choose

Opened documents and unsaved changes are held by the app while you work. Save a copy before closing if you want to retain changes. Original files and saved copies are managed by you in Finder or your chosen storage provider.

If you select a folder managed by iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider may synchronize the files under its own settings and policies. Printing sends the selected content through macOS to the printer or destination you choose. ABLE Tech does not control those providers, printers or separately saved copies.

Apple may process app diagnostics according to your macOS privacy and diagnostic-sharing settings. See Apple's privacy policy for Apple's handling of information.

Support correspondence

If you contact support, ABLE Tech receives the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot and maintain support records. Your email provider also handles the correspondence under its own policies. Please do not send passwords or confidential PDFs.

Retention and your choices

You control the retention and deletion of your original files and saved copies. Removing the app does not delete files saved elsewhere on your Mac or with a file provider. Copies held by a printer, storage provider or recipient must be managed separately.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request, maintain support records and meet applicable obligations. To ask about or request deletion of information you provided to support, contact us using the address below. We may need enough information to identify your correspondence before responding.

Policy updates and contact

We will update the effective date on this page when this policy changes. For privacy questions, contact ABLE Tech LLC at support@abletech9g.com.
