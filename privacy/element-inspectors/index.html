Element Inspectors Privacy Policy

How Element Inspectors handles account information, inspection records, photos and location.

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Element Inspectors is provided by ABLE Tech LLC for authorized inspection work. This policy explains how the iOS app and its connected Element Management workspace handle information.

Information used by the app

  • Account and project information: your assigned name, login name, user identifier, role, inspection discipline, client and project assignments, and account status. Your Element Manager supplies and manages this information.
  • Inspection records: information you enter or attach, including daily reports, photos, captions, documents, field notes, material deliveries, completed bid quantities, timesheets, and related dates, revisions and workflow status.
  • Location and photo metadata: when location access is allowed, the app can record precise coordinates, elevation, location accuracy and capture time for inspection photos. Imported photos may contain their own metadata.
  • Weather and device readings: location is used to request local conditions from Apple Weather. The app can store weather observations and iPhone barometer readings with inspection records.
  • Support information: information you choose to send when asking for help, such as your email address, device model, app version, description of a problem and screenshots.

Why information is used

Information is used to activate assigned access, identify report authors, provide project documents and forms, record inspection work, synchronize authorized project information, support report review and distribution, calculate project progress, and resolve support issues.

On-device storage and Apple services

The app keeps local drafts, photos, downloaded documents, preferences and cached workspace information on your device. Saving locally does not necessarily upload a record. Submitted or explicitly uploaded information is transferred to the connected client workspace using Apple iCloud and CloudKit. A signed-in Apple Account and network access are needed for cloud features. Apple handles Apple Account sign-in; ABLE Tech does not receive your Apple Account password.

Element setup codes and access codes are used to activate and sign in to your assigned workspace. Access-related material is kept using the device Keychain and the app’s cloud access system. Do not share your access code with others.

Apple processes cloud information and location-based weather requests through its services, subject to Apple’s privacy policy. Apple may also provide app diagnostics under your Apple privacy settings.

Who can access information

Uploaded inspection information is available to authorized people within the relevant client and project workflow, including the Element Manager and assigned reviewers. Access depends on workspace permissions and assignments. ABLE Tech uses information to operate and support the service. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the service, users or legal rights.

If you export a PDF, email a report, save a photo or share a file, the recipients and services you choose receive that content. Their handling of those copies is outside the app’s controls.

Advertising and tracking

Element Inspectors does not display advertising, sell personal information, or use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs to track you across apps and websites.

Permissions and your choices

The app requests camera access for inspection photos and document scanning, location access for inspection location and weather, motion access for barometer readings, and permission to add copies of inspection photos to your photo library. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings. Declining a permission can limit the related feature. Use the system photo or file picker to choose content to import.

Signing out ends your app session but does not delete your saved work or previously uploaded records. Copies saved to Photos, Files, email or another service are managed separately.

Retention, corrections and deletion requests

Local information remains until it is removed through available app controls or the app’s local data is deleted. Uploaded client and project records are retained as needed for the organization’s inspection work, recordkeeping obligations, support, legal obligations and disputes. Removing the app or signing out does not remove cloud records or copies already exported or distributed.

Contact your Element Manager to correct your profile, change project access or request removal of organizational records. You may also contact ABLE Tech at support@abletech9g.com for privacy questions, access revocation or deletion assistance. Include the app name and enough information to identify your account and client. Do not send your access code or Apple Account password. We may need to verify your identity and coordinate with the organization responsible for the records. Some records may need to be retained for legal or project recordkeeping requirements; recipients may retain separately distributed copies.

Children

Element Inspectors is a professional inspection tool and is not intended for children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through the service.

Policy updates and contact

We will update the effective date on this page when this policy changes. For privacy questions, contact ABLE Tech LLC at support@abletech9g.com.
