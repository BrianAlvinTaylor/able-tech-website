College4Less QuickCourse Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 6, 2026

College4Less QuickCourse (“QuickCourse,” “the App,” “we,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Accounts

QuickCourse does not require you to create an account with the developer. We do not request your name, email address, phone number, or Apple Account password.

Some optional cloud features require you to be signed in to iCloud. Apple provides the App with a unique CloudKit identifier. The developer does not receive your Apple Account credentials.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your course progress is stored locally on your device. This can include:

Completed lectures

Course progress

Progress-check and quiz results

Final-exam results

App preferences

Downloaded narration resources and generated audio

QuickCourse does not upload your course progress, quiz scores, or final-exam results to the developer’s cloud system.

Removing the App or clearing its data may remove locally stored information.

Optional Cloud Features

QuickCourse uses Apple CloudKit to provide optional surveys, privacy choices, and sponsored opportunities.

If you choose to share demographic information, the App may store:

Your selected age range

Your selected demographic description

Your five-digit ZIP code

A CloudKit-generated user identifier

The App’s identifier

The date the information was provided or updated

Providing this information is optional. You may continue using QuickCourse without sharing demographic information.

General surveys or sponsored opportunities may still appear when demographic sharing is turned off.

If you answer a cloud survey, the App may store:

The survey and question identifiers

Your selected answers

A CloudKit-generated user identifier

The App’s identifier

The response date

The identifier helps prevent repeated survey responses and allows associated cloud data to be deleted.

When demographic sharing is enabled, QuickCourse may also record limited interactions with cloud surveys or sponsored opportunities, such as whether a survey was displayed or completed or whether a sponsored opportunity was displayed or selected.

Aggregate impression and selection counts may be maintained to measure campaign performance.

How Cloud Information Is Used

Cloud information may be used to:

Provide general or relevant surveys

Select sponsored opportunities that may be relevant to an age range, demographic selection, ZIP-code area, or participating app

Prevent surveys from being repeatedly presented

Measure survey participation and sponsored-opportunity performance

Maintain and improve the App and related College4Less services

Cloud information is stored using Apple CloudKit. Apple explains that CloudKit provides developers with a unique identifier without giving them access to the user’s Apple Account information. More information is available in Apple’s privacy information.

Sponsored Opportunities

QuickCourse may display developer-managed sponsored opportunities. These are delivered through our CloudKit system and not through Google AdMob.

We do not sell your demographic information or survey responses to advertisers. The App does not use an advertising identifier to track you across apps or websites.

Selecting a sponsored opportunity may open an external website. Once you leave QuickCourse, that website’s privacy policy and data practices apply.

Kokoro Narration

QuickCourse uses Kokoro speech technology to generate course narration directly on your device.

The text used for narration and the generated narration audio are processed locally and are not uploaded to the developer or to an external speech-generation service.

The App may download the Kokoro model and voice-resource files from GitHub when narration is first prepared. GitHub may receive standard network information associated with that download, such as an IP address and connection information. QuickCourse does not send your course activity, survey answers, voice recordings, or generated narration to GitHub.

Information about GitHub’s practices is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Subscriptions and Payments

QuickCourse offers an auto-renewable subscription through Apple’s App Store.

Apple processes subscription payments and manages billing information. The developer does not receive your complete payment-card or banking information.

The App uses Apple StoreKit to determine whether your subscription is active so that premium content can be unlocked. Apple’s privacy practices apply to App Store purchases.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by:

Apple, through iCloud, CloudKit, StoreKit, and App Store services

GitHub, when the App downloads the static Kokoro model and voice files

Websites you independently choose to open from a sponsored opportunity

We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the App, or our legal rights.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may decline to provide optional demographic information.

When demographic sharing is enabled, the App provides a privacy screen where you can update your information or select “Stop Sharing and Delete Cloud Data.” This removes the demographic profile, survey responses, and linked cloud activity associated with the App’s CloudKit identifier. Local course progress is not affected.

You may also contact us to ask questions or request assistance with cloud-data deletion.

Aggregate campaign counts that no longer identify an individual user may remain after deletion.

Data Retention

Local information remains on your device until it is removed by the App, by you, or when the App’s data is deleted.

Cloud information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the described features, operate the service, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. User-linked cloud information may be deleted through the available privacy controls or by contacting us.

Children’s Privacy

QuickCourse is intended for users age 13 and older. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the App, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and deleted as appropriate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The revised policy will display a new “Last Updated” date.
