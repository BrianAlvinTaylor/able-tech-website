Element Inspectors Support

Help with access, daily reports, photos and project uploads.

Contact support

For technical help with Element Inspectors, contact ABLE Tech LLC. For your setup code, login name, access code, project assignments or inspector role, contact your Element Manager.

Signing in

Your Element Manager provides your client setup code, login name and access code. Enter these in Element Inspectors. Cloud access also requires an Apple Account signed in to iCloud on your device and an internet connection. If access is missing or your assigned project is incorrect, ask your Element Manager to check and upload your access.

Daily report forms

Your Element Manager makes approved forms available for your client, project type and inspection discipline. If a daily form is missing, refresh your workspace and ask the manager to confirm the matching form has been uploaded.

Saved work and uploads

A report or quantity saved on your iPhone has not necessarily reached the cloud. Check the record’s upload status. For completed bid quantities, save the entry and then choose Upload Progress. Only the assigned Bid Progress Inspector can enter and upload completed quantities.

If an upload fails, keep the saved entry, check your internet connection and use Retry Upload. Send support the exact error message if it continues. Do not delete the app while you need unsent work; deleting the app can remove local drafts and files.

Photos, location and weather

Check the app’s permissions in iOS Settings if the camera, photo saving, inspection location or weather is unavailable. Weather requires location access and a working connection to Apple Weather. An afternoon reading is available after an afternoon observation has been recorded.

Sharing reports

Use the report’s PDF sharing or email option to send a copy. Email services may handle large attachments differently, including converting them to file-sharing links. Check the attachment and recipients before sending.

What to include when asking for help

  • Element Inspectors and the app version/build shown in TestFlight or your installed app information.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • A short description of the issue and the steps that led to it.
  • The exact error text or a screenshot with confidential information removed.

Do not send access codes, Apple Account passwords or confidential project documents. Support will ask if more information is needed.

Privacy and data requests

For profile corrections, project access or organizational records, contact your Element Manager. For privacy or deletion assistance, contact ABLE Tech using the support address above. See the privacy policy for details about storage, sharing and retention.
